# Character reference

Put a single clean, front-facing portrait of your character here as
`character-reference.jpg`.

This is the most important file for making the account look like one person
rather than a different woman every day. Image providers with an
image-conditioning model (IPAdapter or InstantID on ComfyUI, an image input
field on Replicate) use it to hold the face steady across posts; the locked
`persona.appearance` text and the fixed seed do the rest.

What works best:

- head and shoulders, face unobstructed, both eyes visible
- even lighting, no heavy shadow across the face
- neutral or slight expression
- at least 1024px on the short edge
- one person in frame, no crop through the chin or forehead

`influencer doctor` reports whether the file is present and readable.

## One rule

The character must be one you have the rights to use — generated, commissioned,
or otherwise yours. Do not use a photograph of a real person. An automated
account posting daily in someone's likeness is impersonation regardless of what
the captions say, and the disclosure label does not make it acceptable.
